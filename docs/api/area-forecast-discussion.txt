Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
422 PM PDT Sat Aug 15 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Slight chance of showers and thunderstorms over the mountains and
high desert this afternoon. High pressure will begin to expand
across the area through the upcoming week. This will lead to a
warming trend over the next few days with temperatures remaining
slightly above normal thereafter. This will also lead to a drier
weather pattern with a shallower marine layer closer to the coast
each night and morning.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...


...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION...

A trough is draped across the State, leading to temperatures
slightly below normal today. This pattern of greater onshore flow
and cooler weather also allowed for a deeper marine layer that
covered inland valleys of Orange and San Diego Counties earlier
this morning. We`ll give the monsoon another try later this
afternoon as some mid-level moisture is seen from this morning`s
sounding. Cumulus building over the mountain ridges into the high
desert may be able to produce a few showers or weak thunderstorms,
accompanied by locally gusty winds.

High pressure off to the east will slowly expand across the region
by Sunday into the middle of next week. This will bring a drying
trend on Sunday, with chances for any showers across the
mountains near 10 percent or less. Temperatures for Sunday will be
very similar to those of today. Models continue to show high
pressure expanding and strengthening over our area by Monday to
around Wednesday or Thursday of next week. Temperatures will
increase 2-4 degrees each day away from the coastal regions,
where moderate to locally major heat risk is possible for inland
valleys and desert regions. The strengthening weather system will
also push the marine layer down further, limiting the marine layer
to cover areas near the coast each night and morning.

Model ensembles show fair agreement on the high pressure system
sticking around the Desert Southwest into the latter half of next
week. The elongated form of the high will eventually become
amplified over the region later in the week. How much
amplification and exact location of the high is still to be
determined. Depending on it`s position, it may be able to entrain
some monsoonal moisture back into our region as early as
Thursday, possibly lingering into next weekend, which would
trigger showers/storms for the mountains and deserts.

&&

.AVIATION...
152330Z....Coast/Valleys...Very patchy low clouds are starting to
develop over nearshore waters this afternoon. Low clouds will expand
and slowly push into coastal San Diego County starting 01-03z.
Clouds slowly push inland and northward into Orange County
overnight, reaching up to 20 miles inland by 12z. Bases will be
slightly lower than last night, around 1500-2000 ft MSL. Clouds may
briefly push into the western Inland Empire 13-16z, with a 40-50%
chance for cigs at KONT. Minor vis reductions (3-5SM) possible for
elevated inland valleys (KONT, KRNM). Clouds clear toward the
coastline 15-18z Sun with VFR conditions expected for Sunday
afternoon.

Mountains/Deserts...VFR conditions prevail through the TAF period.
SCT-BKN cu AOA 10,000ft MSL expected this afternoon with a slight
chance (10-20%) for TSRA over the Riverside County mountains and in
the high deserts.

&&

.MARINE...
Gusty northwest winds 20-23 knots near San Clemente Island expected
Sunday and Monday afternoon. Otherwise, no hazardous marine
conditions are expected through Thursday.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.


&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...None.
PZ...None.
