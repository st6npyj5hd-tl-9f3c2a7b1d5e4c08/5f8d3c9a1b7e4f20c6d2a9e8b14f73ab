AFDSGX

Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
1118 PM PDT Fri Jul 31 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
The heat is on across SoCal as high pressure strengthens over the
area through the weekend, leading to areas of moderate to major
heat risk. The marine layer will stay thin through this period,
where night and morning low clouds will cover the coast each day.
The area of high pressure will weaken some into next week and may
move further east. This pattern will bring slightly cooler weather
with a minimal chance for rainfall over the mountains.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...

...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION...

The warming trend continues as the center of a growing high pressure
system moves over Arizona today. The system will peak in intensity
and be centered near the southern Colorado River Valley by Saturday.
This is when we can expect the hottest temperatures with highs well
into the 90s for valleys and mountains of Orange and San Diego
Counties and over 115 degrees across the lower deserts. The high
weakens slightly on Sunday, but temperatures will still remain hot
away from the immediate coast.

With the hot temperatures, please severely limit or postpone outdoor
recreation if you are in areas away from the coast, since
temperatures will be well into the 70s and 80s by the mid-morning
hours. For those without A/C, make sure to go to air conditioned
areas and drink plenty of water. Warm and humid nights in the
60s/70s/80s will lead to less relief from the heat as well.

By early next week, the high weakens subtly. The position of where
the high will go is still somewhat in flux. This will bring a
degree or two of cooling for inland areas each day, but warm
temperatures will still remain. Temperatures will stay near to
slightly above normal by Monday onward next week. The high may go
into a position that will bring in a subtle return of monsoonal
moisture and greater humidity to the region, but rain chances
still seem slim near 15% or less across our mountain areas by
Wednesday into the latter half of next week.

&&

.AVIATION...
010600Z....Coasts...Very patchy low clouds based around 1200ft MSL
have developed offshore and along the coast. Brief CIGs have already
been observed at KSAN and KCRQ and intermittent CIGs will likely
persist through the next several hours as these patchy clouds move
in and out. Guidance is indicating that clouds should become more
consistent in coverage closer to 10-12z, with bases closer to 0800-
1100ft MSL and about 10 miles of inland extent. VIS restrictions to
3-5SM expected over mesas and higher coastal terrain when clouds are
present. Clouds will scatter out around 16z Saturday morning, with
VFR prevailing into the afternoon. Similarly patchy low clouds will
form locally along the coast after 06z Sunday, initially based at
800-1100 ft MSL.

.Valleys/Mountains/Deserts...VFR conditions prevails throughout
the period with FEW high clouds AOA 20000 feet MSL.

&&

.MARINE...
No hazardous marine conditions are expected through Wednesday.

&&

.BEACHES...
A southerly swell from now Tropical Storm Genevieve will generate
surf of 4-6 feet with sets up to 7 feet for south facing beaches
today and tonight before diminishing Saturday. This will create
hazardous conditions for inexperienced swimmers. In addition, the
high tide tonight will be near 6 feet. The combination of the high
tides and elevated surf increases the risk of coastal flooding. For
more details, check the Coastal Hazard Message.

&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Coastal Flood Advisory until 3 AM PDT Saturday for Orange County
     Coastal Areas-San Diego County Coastal Areas.

     High Surf Advisory until 5 PM PDT Saturday for Orange County
     Coastal Areas-San Diego County Coastal Areas.

     Heat Advisory until 8 PM PDT Sunday for Orange County Coastal
     Areas-San Bernardino County Mountains-San Diego County
     Coastal Areas.

     Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT Sunday for Apple and Lucerne
     Valleys-Orange County Inland Areas-Riverside County
     Mountains-San Bernardino and Riverside County Valleys-The
     Inland Empire-San Diego County Mountains-San Diego County
     Valleys-Santa Ana Mountains and Foothills.

     Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT Monday for Coachella Valley-
     San Diego County Deserts-San Gorgonio Pass near Banning.

PZ...None.
