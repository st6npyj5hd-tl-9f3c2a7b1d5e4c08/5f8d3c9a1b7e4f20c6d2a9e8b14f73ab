AFDSGX

Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
347 AM PDT Thu Sep 10 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
The tropical air mass of former Hurricane Marie that has brought
this hot humid heat wave to the area this week will transition
over the weekend with a trough of low pressure bringing drier
southwest flow aloft and onshore lower level flow. High
temperatures will not be quite as hot today for the coast into the
western valleys with the cooling then spreading inland for Friday
through Sunday with not much change for early next week. There is
still a slight chance of showers and thunderstorms for the
mountains for this afternoon.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...

.SHORT TERM (Today through Saturday)...
It was a very warm start on Wednesday with low temperatures for
coast and valleys mostly in the 70s to mid 80s. High temperatures
on Wednesday of 105 to 110 were widespread for inland coastal
areas into the western valleys. There was less coverage of
thunderstorms on Wednesday with the strongest recorded
thunderstorms wind gust to 50 mph at Pauma Creek in the Pauma
Valley in San Diego County at 1:40 PM.

As of 3 AM early this morning, temperatures are still in the very
warm 70s to mid 80s for the coast and valleys, but are a few to
around 5 degrees cooler than 24 hours ago for coastal areas into
the western valleys. High temperatures will not be quite as hot
for coastal areas into the western valleys today, but still with
areas of Major to Extreme HeatRisk for inland coastal areas into
the western valleys. High temperatures for today will range from
80s at the beaches to the upper 90s to 108 for the Inland Empire
with the lower deserts 110 to 114. High temperatures for the
mountains and deserts will be a few degrees warmer than Wednesday.

Cooling will spread inland across all of southwestern California
on Friday with the cooling continuing through the weekend. By
Saturday, moderate or greater HeatRisk will be mostly confined to
the lower deserts. High temperatures on Saturday will range from
the mid 70s to lower 80s near the coast to the upper 80s to mid
90s for the Inland Empire with 104 to 108 for the lower deserts.
Low temperatures early Saturday morning will be mostly in the 60s
and lower 70s for the coast and valleys.

&&

.LONG TERM (Sunday through Wednesday)...
A low pressure system moving inland through the western states
will maintain drier southwest flow aloft. High temperatures on
Sunday will be as much as 4 to 8 degrees below average for the
Inland Empire. High temperatures on Sunday will range from the mid
to upper 70s near the coast to the mid to upper 80s for the Inland
Empire with 100 to 104 for the lower deserts. Low temperatures
will be mostly in the 50s for the inland valleys with the coast
into the western valley in the 60s. Patchy night and morning
coastal low clouds will spread into the far western valleys at
times.

&&

.AVIATION...
100530Z...VFR conditions expected across the region through
Thursday, with SCT to locally BKN clouds between 10-20 kft. ISO
SHRA/TSRA over mountains 19-00Z Thursday, with CB clouds based 8-12
kft MSL and tops rising to 30 kft. Localized gusty winds and heavy
rain in and near any TSRA.

&&

.MARINE...
No hazardous marine conditions are expected through Monday.

&&

.BEACHES...
High tides peaking earlier this evening are not expected to return
to these hazardous levels and the coastal flood advisory will be
allowed to expire tonight. Check the Coastal Hazard Message for
details.

&&

.CLIMATE...
The high temperature of 112 at Escondido on Wednesday was not only a
new record for the date, it tied for the fourth highest daily
temperature ever recorded at Escondido with a period of record
going back to 1893. It was also the latest calendar date for 112
or higher to occur, later than the 115 on September 6th, 2020 and
112 on September 6th, 2024. The heat index at Escondido on
Wednesday was highest at 119 at 1:15 PM with a temperature of 111
and a dewpoint of 68.

The low temperature of 81 at Escondido on Wednesday was also a
record for the warmest low temperature for the date, exceeding the
previous record for the date of 80 in 1984.

The average temperature for Escondido on Wednesday of 96.5 was
the warmest daily average temperature ever recorded at Escondido
with records going back to 1893, exceeding the 95 on September
6th, 2020 and July 22nd, 2006.

San Diego has now had high temperatures exceeding 90 for the past
four days (95 on the 6th and 7th, 93 on the 8th, and 94 on the
9th). The most recent time that occurred was during 2020 from
September 28th through October 2nd. High temperatures of 90 or
higher at San Diego for 4 or more consecutive days has now only
happened at San Diego 15 times with records going back to 1874.
The longest stretch of high temperatures of 90 or higher at San
Diego was in 1939 from September 17th-22nd in the heat wave that
preceded the tropical storm that made landfall in Long Beach on
the 25th.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.

&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT this evening for Orange
     County Coastal Areas-Orange County Inland Areas-San
     Bernardino and Riverside County Valleys-The Inland Empire-
     San Diego County Coastal Areas-San Diego County Valleys-
     Santa Ana Mountains and Foothills.

     Heat Advisory until 8 PM PDT this evening for Riverside County
     Mountains-San Diego County Mountains.

     Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT Friday for Coachella Valley-
     San Diego County Deserts-San Gorgonio Pass near Banning.

PZ...None.

&&
