Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
955 AM PST Fri Nov 20 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Isolated light showers across San Diego County ending by mid-
morning. Cool but dry weather through this weekend. A weak
weather system will bring a slight chance of light showers along
and west of the mountains Sunday night through Monday morning.
Brief warming Tuesday before another potential system on
Wednesday through early Thursday, though confidence in the track
and intensity remains low. Warmer and drier towards the end of the
week under weak offshore flow.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...


...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION...

Morning water vapor imagery shows an upper level low moving into
northern Nevada and a trough extending south into Southern
California. While the main band of precipitation has moved out,
residual showers continue over the coastal waters and into San
Diego County. Expect these showers to come to an end by mid-
morning as the trough departs and drier air filters in. The
remainder of the day will be cool and dry with highs in the 60s
across the coastal basin, 40s in the mountains, mid 50s in the
high desert, and low 70s in the low desert.

A short wave on the back side of the low, currently along the
coast near the OR/CA border, will rotate down through interior CA
later today, passing by to our north and east. Surface high
pressure building over the Great Basin behind this wave will drive
very weak offshore flow here on Saturday morning. This will lead
to slightly warmer and drier weather. Onshore flow returns on
Sunday with little change in temperatures. Overall fair weather
this weekend with some high clouds at times.

Another upper level trough quickly moves through California Sunday
night into Monday morning. Several members of each of the global
ensembles remain dry with this trough (about 26% of the total
ensemble space). Most of the members have at least light
precipitation, so have a slight chance for the mountains westward
Sunday night into Monday morning, though any accumulation will
likely be limited to a few hundredths. Snow levels will be near
6500 ft, but given the lack of moisture expect only a few
flurries. Upper level ridging moves over late Monday and Tuesday
for drier and warmer weather with another brief round of weak
offshore flow Tuesday morning.

Differences in the upper level pattern really begin to emerge as
we head into Wednesday. The deterministic GFS still becomes highly
amplified, bringing a deep trough into So Cal (and another deep
closed low over the Southeast US) by late Wednesday. This
solution, which would result in much colder and wetter weather,
remains an outlier and isn`t supported by the EC/Canadian
ensembles or even most of the GEFS ensemble members. Most of the
solutions point to more of a zonal flow pattern with the
moisture/precipitation aimed at Northern California. For now the
official forecast has a 15-20% chance of precipitation on
Wednesday. Once this trough passes, upper level high pressure
begins to build in from the west. Weak offshore flow could develop
as early as Thanksgiving morning, strengthening into Friday. For
Thanksgiving Day, high temperatures are forecast to be anywhere
from 2 to 7 degrees above normal, warming to around 5 to 10
degrees above normal by Friday.

&&

.AVIATION...
201800Z...SCT-BKN clouds around 3500-5000 feet MSL prevails across
coastal San Diego County today with VFR conditions expected across
the board. A stray shower is possible along the coastal areas into
the early afternoon, but there is less than a 5% chance. Westerly
winds gusting 25-30 kts through mountain gaps into adjacent deserts
through 23Z.

&&

.MARINE...
Winds and seas remain elevated in the outer waters into this
evening. Wind gusts 25-30 kts are expected with choppy, locally
steep, seas of 6-8 feet. A Small Craft Advisory remains in effect
until 9 PM this evening for the outer waters and contains more
details. No additional marine hazards are expected thereafter
through Tuesday.

&&

.BEACHES...
Marginally elevated surf has come down a little bit, with highest
sets up to 5-6 feet lingering today. The Beach Hazard Statement has
been allowed to expire as a result.

King Tides are expected to peak next week, but chances of tides
exceeding 7 feet start as early as Saturday Nov 21. Morning high
tides may even exceed 8 feet next Tuesday through Thursday bringing
widespread moderate impacts.

&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Coastal Flood Watch from late Sunday night through Friday
     afternoon for Orange County Coastal Areas-San Diego County
     Coastal Areas.

PZ...Small Craft Advisory until 9 PM PST this evening for Waters from
     San Mateo Point to the Mexican Border Extending 10 to 60 nm
     out including San Clemente Island.


&&
