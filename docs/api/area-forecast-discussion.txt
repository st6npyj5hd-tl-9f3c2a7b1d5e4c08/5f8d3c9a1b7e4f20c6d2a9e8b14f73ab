Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
237 AM PDT Sun Oct 4 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Hot days and warm nights, mainly west of the mountains, will
continue through Thursday. Locally gusty Santa Ana winds will
continue today, strongest near the coastal mountain slopes. Today
will likely be the hottest day, but confidence is improving that
Friday will be noticeably less hot and next weekend will be
cooler.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...


...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION...

This morning...Temperatures are near or a few degrees higher than
at this time yesterday under clear skies. Offshore flow continues,
with the windy locations reporting easterly winds gusting 20-35
mph at this hour. Several locations reported broken or tied high
temperature records. Please see our Record Event Report.

Today will likely be the hottest day of the week as high pressure
aloft and gusty Santa Ana winds combine to produce daytime high
temperatures ranging from the mid 90s to low triple digits in the
coastal areas and inland valleys. These temperatures are about
15-25 degrees above normal. Daytime high temperatures remain well
above normal west of the mountains through Thursday with moderate
to high HeatRisk, although slight cooling from the sea breeze will
incrementally spread farther inland each day as the offshore flow
weakens.

The high pressure ridge aloft will weaken a little on Monday and
conditions become a little less stable. Enough for there to be
clouds and a slight chance for showers Monday afternoon over the
San Bernardino and Riverside County mountains. Any precipitation
that occurs will likely be localized and light. The earliest that
any model guidance shows marine layer clouds/fog returning to the
coastal areas is Wed night/Thu morning, but the models have
historically been slow with the return of the marine layer after a
Santa Ana event so we`ll be monitoring this closely.

Most model solutions are now indicating noticeable cooling on
Friday as the high pressure ridge weakens and gets displaced to
the south and east in response to a low pressure trough moving in
from the northwest. Conditions will still be warm west of the
mountains, with daytime high temperatures 10-15 degrees above
normal. The coastal areas will likely see high temps in the 80s
while the inland valleys will still see high temps in the mid to
upper 90s.

For next weekend...Significant cooling is likely as a majority of
ensemble members show a low pressure trough deepening along the
west coast. Current indications are for temperatures lowering to a
few degrees above normal on Saturday then lowering further to a
few degrees below normal on Sunday. A slim majority of ensemble
members also show slight chances for widespread light rain on
Sunday but confidence is still low.

&&

.AVIATION...
040530Z...Coast/western valleys: SKC and VFR through the period.
There is a 20-40% chance E/SE winds gusting 10-20 kts could reach
parts of the coast from 15-19z Sun, including at KSAN and KCRQ
(highest chance at KCRQ). Winds turn onshore W/NW late Sun morning.

Inland Valleys/Foothills/Mountains: E/SE winds increase and expand
westward to valleys in the early morning hours 12-15z, peaking
between 16-20z with gusts to 15-35 kts. MOD UDDFS expected over
coastal slopes. Winds gradually turn westerly this afternoon through
the valleys as the sea breeze moves inland.

Otherwise...Clear skies through the TAF period.

&&

.MARINE...
No hazardous marine conditions are expected through Thursday.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.


&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT Thursday for Orange County
     Coastal Areas-Orange County Inland Areas-San Bernardino and
     Riverside County Valleys-The Inland Empire-San Diego County
     Coastal Areas-San Diego County Valleys-Santa Ana Mountains
     and Foothills.

PZ...None.

&&
