Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
511 PM PDT Sun Oct 25 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Near to slightly above average temperatures through Monday, then
gradually cooler for the middle to end of the week. Unsettled
weather expected for the end of the week with increased winds and
chance of precipitation. Tides will remain elevated through much
of this week.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...


...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION...

Visible satellite at 11 AM was showing clear skies across Southern
California. Highs today are expected to be in the upper 70s to upper
80s for most locations, with spots in the mountains in the 50 to 60
degree range. The pressure gradient between San Diego and Tonopah,
NV was still weakly offshore this morning. Onshore flow is expected
to return Monday as an upper level low pressure system moves into
the Pacific Northwest. Marine layer low clouds have a small
chance of returning to some coastal locations Monday morning, with
higher confidence for low clouds returning by Tuesday morning.
Once marine layer low clouds and areas of fog return, they will be
present each night and morning through the week.

High temperatures Monday will be similar to today`s. High
temperatures in coastal areas will be 5 to 10 degrees above average,
with temperatures closer to average further inland. By Wednesday,
forecast has widespread below average temperatures. Temperatures are
forecast to remain below average through the remainder of the
week.

Global ensembles continue to indicate unsettled weather for
the end of the week into the first days of November. There
remains a lot of uncertainty in the details, which will ultimately
be dictated by how far south the upper level low off the coast of
the Pacific Northwest tracks. Rain chances are in the forecast as
early as Friday. In addition to precipitation, cooler, cloudier,
and windier conditions are expected.

&&

.AVIATION...
260000Z...Mostly VFR conditions across the region through the
period. SCT cumulus over mts through this evening. 20% chance for
very patchy low clouds based 500-1000 ft MSL over nearshore waters
and along the coastline tonight. No impacts expected for coastal TAF
sites at this time.

&&

.MARINE...
Winds gusts near 20 knots expected in the outer coastal waters
Monday afternoon and evening. Otherwise, no hazardous marine
conditions are expected through Thursday.

&&

.BEACHES...
Morning high tides will create significant coastal flooding through
Thursday afternoon. Tides will be anywhere from 1 to 1.7 feet above
astronomical predictions, with morning high tides approaching and potentially
exceeding 8 feet starting Monday morning. While surf will not be
elevated, these will be near if not record-breaking tides. A Coastal
Flood Warning is in effect for the entire Orange and San Diego
coastline through Thursday morning.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.


&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Coastal Flood Warning until 1 PM PDT Thursday for Orange County
     Coastal Areas-San Diego County Coastal Areas.

PZ...None.

&&

$$

AVIATION/MARINE/BEACHES...Westerink
