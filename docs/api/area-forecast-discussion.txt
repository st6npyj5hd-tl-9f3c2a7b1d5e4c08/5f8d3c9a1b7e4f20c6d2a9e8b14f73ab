Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
524 PM PDT Wed Jul 22 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Strengthening high pressure aloft into the weekend will bring very
hot days for inland areas and warm humid nights except for the
highest elevations in the mountains for Thursday into the weekend
with record overnight warmth for many areas for Friday and
Saturday. Monday and Tuesday will cool a few degrees each day, but
high pressure as strong or stronger than late this week could
return near the end of next week.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...


...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION...

High pressure aloft will expand westward today and will build into
the weekend. This means high temperatures will continue and increase
over the next few days.

High temperatures for the coastal areas today will be a few degrees
cooler than yesterdays highs, while high temperatures in the
mountains and Inland Empire will warm 5 to 10 degrees. Thursday,
highs will warm 5 to locally 10 degrees in the valleys and in the
coastal mountain slopes with little to no change for high
temperatures Friday and Saturday. This will bring seasonal
temperatures 5 to 10 degrees above average for most of the area
Thursday through Saturday.

Elevated overnight lows will offer little to no reprieve from the
daytime heat. Coastal areas and inland valleys will see overnight
lows predominately in the 70s, upper 80s to upper 90s in the low
deserts, and mid 70s to mid 80s for the high deserts.

These high afternoon temperatures and elevated lows will bring
widespread moderate HeatRisk to most of the area with major HeatRisk
in the Inland Empire, San Diego mountains and south deserts today,
and to include the high deserts Friday through Sunday. Major to
extreme HeatRisk indicates that anyone may be negatively impacted by
the heat if the proper precautions are not taken. Make sure to stay
hydrated, stay cool, avoid heavy activity and direct sunlight and
check on the elderly, children and pets.

Coastal low clouds are expected to be patchy in nature and stick
close to the coast through Saturday. Some high clouds will move
across the area later this afternoon through the evening. A slight
increase in monsoonal moisture will create mid-level clouds over the
mountains Thursday and Friday with a 10-20% chance for light rain in
the mountains for San Bernardino and Riverside Counties.

High temperatures will continue into early next week with some
slight cooling for daily high and low temperatures by mid-week. The
ECMWF and GFS show high pressure aloft returning by the end of next
week that is as strong or possibly stronger.

&&

.AVIATION...
230000Z....Coast/Valleys...SCT low clouds are lining the SD County
coast this evening. Cigs will struggle to develop most likely until
after 08z Thu, when there is moderate confidence for BKN cigs over
SD County coastal areas up to 15 miles inland. Bases most likely 500-
900 ft MSL. Patchy vis reductions (from BR/HZ): 4-6SM (locally 0-
3SM) over elevated coastal terrain and valleys along the I-15
corridor. Orange County will struggle to develop cigs until after
11z, from south to north. Less than 40% chance for more than 1 hr of
cigs overnight at KSNA. Clouds scatter out 15-17z.


Mountains/Deserts...FEW high clouds tonight and small afternoon
cumulus over mountain peaks after 21z Thu. Otherwise, VFR conditions
prevail through the TAF period.

&&

.MARINE...
Northwesterly winds with gusts 20-25 kts in the outer waters near
San Clemente Island to continue this evening and again Thursday
afternoon. Additionally, a mixed swell will cause steep and choppy
seas 6 to 7 feet on Thursday and Friday in the outer waters.

&&

.BEACHES...
A 3 to 4 feet south swell from 190 degrees with 13-15 second period
will bring surf of 5 to 7 feet to area beaches Thursday through
Sunday. Highest surf will be on south-southwest facing beaches. In
addition to elevated surf, tides are expected to increase starting
Friday peaking at 5.5 to 6.5 feet MLLW by early next week. The
combination of surf and tides will result in minor coastal flooding
during high tides.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.


&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Beach Hazards Statement from 5 AM PDT Thursday through Sunday
     evening for Orange County Coastal Areas-San Diego County
     Coastal Areas.

     Heat Advisory from 10 AM Thursday to 8 PM PDT Monday for Orange
     County Coastal Areas-San Diego County Coastal Areas.

     Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT Monday for Coachella Valley-
     San Bernardino and Riverside County Valleys-The Inland
     Empire-San Diego County Deserts-San Diego County Mountains-
     San Gorgonio Pass near Banning.

     Heat Advisory until 8 PM PDT Monday for Orange County Inland
     Areas-Riverside County Mountains-San Bernardino County
     Mountains-San Diego County Valleys-Santa Ana Mountains and
     Foothills.

     Extreme Heat Warning from 10 AM Thursday to 8 PM PDT Monday for
     Apple and Lucerne Valleys.

PZ...Small Craft Advisory until 9 PM PDT Thursday for Waters from San
     Mateo Point to the Mexican Border Extending 10 to 60 nm out
     including San Clemente Island.


&&
