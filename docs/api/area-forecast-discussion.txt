Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
159 AM PDT Fri Oct 30 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Scattered, mostly light showers will continue for the coast and
valleys onto the coastal slopes of the mountains into this
morning with more widely scattered showers possible at times for
this afternoon into Saturday morning. More widespread showers
will return Saturday afternoon or evening with showers possibly
becoming heavy at times for late Saturday night into Sunday
morning. Showers are expected to decrease on Sunday afternoon and
end by Monday morning. Dry and warmer for Monday and Tuesday with
high temperatures warming to around average on Tuesday. Around 5
degrees cooler for Wednesday and Thursday with a chance of
showers.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...

.SHORT TERM (Today through Sunday)...
Scattered showers across the coast and valleys onto the coastal
slopes of the mountains have mostly produced 0.10 inch or less of
rainfall for Thursday evening through 2 AM today with isolated
totals to 0.25 to 0.35 inch.

Scattered, mostly light showers will continue for the coast and
valleys onto the coastal slopes of the mountains into this
morning with more widely scattered showers possible at times for
this afternoon into Saturday with additional rainfall mostly 0.10
inch to locally 0.25 inch into Saturday morning.

More widespread showers will return Saturday afternoon or evening
with showers possibly becoming heavy at times for late Saturday
night into Sunday morning. Showers are expected to decrease on
Sunday afternoon and end by Monday morning.

For Saturday morning through Sunday night from the coast to the
mountains, the chance for 0.10 inch or more of rainfall is mostly
80 to 90 percent.

For Saturday morning through Sunday night from the coast to the
mountains, the chance for 1 inch or more of rainfall ranges from
30 percent for eastern Los Angeles County to 65 to 70 percent for
southern San Diego County.

For Saturday morning through Sunday night from the coast to the
mountains, the chance for 2 inches or more of rainfall ranges
from 10 percent for Orange and southwestern San Bernardino
Counties to 35 to 45 percent over far southern San Diego County.

&&

.LONG TERM (Monday through Thursday)...
Dry and warmer for Monday and Tuesday with high temperatures on
Tuesday warming to around average. High temperatures on Tuesday
for the coast and valleys will be mostly in the 70s, for the
mountains in the mid 40s to mid 60s, and in the lower deserts
around 80.

Around 5 degrees cooler for Wednesday and Thursday with a chance
of showers as a pair of low pressure systems move inland into
California.

&&

.AVIATION...
300630Z...BKN clouds based 3000-5000 ft from the coast to the mtns
with SCT-BKN high clouds near 8000-1000 ft regionwide through Friday
afternoon. Patchy -DZ and ISO/SCT -SHRA will continue through the
night with the greatest coverage of -SHRA over coastal areas. CIGs
will locally fall to 2500 feet MSL in -SHRA along with local minor
vis reductions especially near the coast. -SHRA diminishes and low
clouds become more FEW-SCT in coverage 14-18z. ISO-SCT -SHRA
redevelop Friday afternoon for the coastal basin with highest
coverage in Orange County. Coverage of BKN low clouds increases
again after 00z Sat, particularly for coastal areas with ISO -SHRA
becoming more confined to coastal areas as well.

&&

.MARINE...
No hazardous marine conditions are expected through Saturday. As a
storm system moves through the coastal waters this weekend, shifting
winds and seas are likely to increase by Sunday morning, possibly to
hazardous levels, lingering into Monday. There is also a slight
chance of thunderstorms Saturday night and Sunday morning. Stay
tuned for updates.

&&

.BEACHES...
A Coastal Flood Advisory is in effect until 2 PM Friday. Friday`s
high tide is expected to peak around 7.25 feet near noon Friday
which could produce minor coastal flooding as surf is expected to be
around 3-5 feet.

The southwesterly swell (3 ft at 15 seconds from 200 degrees)
responsible for the marginally elevated surf through the weekend
will persist into Monday. As a storm system moves into the area for
Sunday/Monday, increased winds could help bolster localized wave
sets to 6 feet. While high tides will continue to wane into the
weekend, tides will remain over 6 feet which could still lead to
minor tidal overflow if breaking waves approach 4-6 feet.

Reported forecast tides have been corrected up by 1.4-1.6ft based on
recent trends in tidal observations.

&&

.CLIMATE...
For San Diego, 5 of the 9 highest water levels ever recorded have
occurred on Sunday through Thursday of this week with the highest
on Tuesday. The water level at the peak of the highest tide on
Saturday near midday will be around 1 foot lower than the highest
water level on Thursday.

Highest water levels at San Diego:

1)  Oct 27, 2026:  2.80 feet MHHW (8.52 feet MLLW)
2)  Oct 26, 2026:  2.72 feet MHHW (8.44 feet MLLW)
3)  Oct 28, 2026:  2.71 feet MHHW (8.43 feet MLLW)
4t) Jul 14, 2026:  2.54 feet MHHW (8.26 feet MLLW)
4t) Nov 25, 2015:  2.54 feet MHHW (8.26 feet MLLW)
6)  Aug 02, 2023:  2.53 feet MHHW (8.25 feet MLLW)
7)  Oct 29, 2026:  2.49 feet MHHW (8.21 feet MLLW)
8)  Dec 13, 2012:  2.45 feet MHHW (8.17 feet MLLW)
9)  Oct 25, 2026:  2.40 feet MHHW (8.12 feet MLLW)

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.

&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Coastal Flood Advisory until 2 PM PDT this afternoon for Orange
     County Coastal Areas-San Diego County Coastal Areas.

PZ...None.

&&

$$
