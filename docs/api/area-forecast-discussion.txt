Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
1246 AM PDT Mon Jul 27 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
A gradual cooling trend begins today and will continue through
Wednesday, bringing temperatures around 5 degrees above average
by midweek. The upper level ridge will shift west again towards
the end of the week and restrengthen with increasing confidence
that this setup could provide us with the hottest temperatures of
this summer thus far, especially by Saturday. Some monsoonal
moisture transiting over the region by later in the week and into
the upcoming weekend may also provide a slight chance of afternoon
showers and thunderstorms for our mountains and deserts.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...

Today through tomorrow night...

Low clouds have continued to struggle to form overnight, with only
some patchy areas of low ceilings and fog, mainly along the coast of
northern San Diego County, and around the Oceanside to Camp
Pendleton districts. Elsewhere, skies have continued to clear.
Temperatures still remain quite mild at midnight, with mostly low
70s at this hour (midnight).

The ridge is beginning to shift a little further east today as
there will be further influence by an area of troughing upstream,
which is going to continue to usher in relatively drier air into
the mid levels, and likely limit the chance of any thunderstorm
development this afternoon as the mid-level moisture becomes more
suppressed and confined over Arizona. This troughing will also
allow for a very gradual cooling to take place today through
tomorrow, although highs will still be running around 5 to 10
degrees above average for most locations. The marine layer will
likely struggle again to develop tonight, and remain mostly in
place along the coastal areas of San Diego County, then slowly
extend further into some of the inland areas closer to daybreak.

Wednesday through the upcoming weekend...

Slight cooling will continue through Wednesday, then the troughing
upstream will begin to weaken and allow for the upper level ridge to
further strengthen and expand back towards the west. This will allow
for temperatures to increase again well back above the seasonal
average for this time of year, by as much as 15 degrees for some
locations, especially within the Inland Empire. By Saturday, there
could be several locations within the IE topping out around 110 and
above, and likely warranting the issuance of more heat products at
that time. If planning to be outdoors for this upcoming weekend, it
would be wise to prepare for this heat by ensuring that there are
plenty of drinking fluids, as the combination of the increase
heat could put those sensitive to heat as risk of dehydration.
There will also be a better push of monsoonal waves transitioning
around the plateau high located around the Four Corners region by
later this week, which will allow for influxes of mid-level
moisture to move in over the region and possibly allow for a few
showers and afternoon development of thunderstorms over the
mountains and deserts, yet this will all be decided on the timing
of these pulses of moisture as they transition over our area.
Otherwise, hot and dry conditions will continue to be the case
into the upcoming weekend.

&&

.AVIATION...
270600Z....Coast/Valleys...Patchy low clouds based 1100-1400 ft MSL
have developed just offshore and will continue to push inland and up
the coast through 12z. VIS reduced along elevated coastal terrain
and locally in elevated valleys up to 15 miles inland. Low clouds
mostly scatter 16-17z. 30% chance that brief cigs possible at the
immediate coast after 21z as low clouds begin redeveloping early.
Otherwise, VFR conditions prevail Monday afternoon until another
round of low clouds push inland likely after 04z with similar bases.

Mountains/Deserts...VFR prevails with FEW-BKN mid-level clouds
around 10-15k ft MSL for Monday. A few stray showers are possible
over the mountains and into the adjacent deserts Monday afternoon.

&&

.MARINE...
Calm conditions into the early half of next week with waves 3 to 5
feet and afternoon wind gusts near 15 kts or less through Tuesday.
Waves increase in the outer waters mid to late week with waves
expected around 5 to 7 feet.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.


&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Beach Hazards Statement through Wednesday afternoon for Orange
     County Coastal Areas-San Diego County Coastal Areas.

     Heat Advisory until 8 PM PDT this evening for Orange County
     Coastal Areas-Orange County Inland Areas-Riverside County
     Mountains-San Bernardino County Mountains-San Bernardino
     and Riverside County Valleys-The Inland Empire-San Diego
     County Coastal Areas-San Diego County Mountains-San Diego
     County Valleys-Santa Ana Mountains and Foothills.

     Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT this evening for Apple and
     Lucerne Valleys-Coachella Valley-San Diego County Deserts-
     San Gorgonio Pass near Banning.

PZ...None.

&&
