AFDSGX

Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
437 PM PDT Tue Oct 27 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Cooling will spread inland as we approach the weekend, with high
temperatures on Sunday around 10 to 15 degrees below average for
inland areas. Chances for showers will begin increasing on Friday
and be highest late Saturday into early Sunday. Chances for
rainfall will be around 45 to 55 percent for 0.50 inch or more
from the coast to the mountains, with a 35 to 40 percent chance
for 1 inches or more. Tides will remain elevated through much of
this week with near record water levels resulting in coastal
flooding.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...


...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION...

Sunny this afternoon, with temperatures near or a little lower
than at this time yesterday except in the mountains where
temperatures are generally a few degrees higher.

A gradual cooling trend will continue through Thursday under zonal
flow aloft ahead of low pressure systems approaching from the west
and northwest. Daytime high temperatures will be mostly 5 to 10
degrees below normal. High temperatures on Thursday for the coast and
valleys will be mostly in the low to mid 70s, for the mountains
in the mid 40s to mid 60s, and for the lower deserts in the low
to mid 80s.

Clouds will begin increasing from the west tonight becoming
widespread by mid-morning on Wednesday as a closed upper low to
our west becomes an open wave as it gets absorbed into the flow
on the south side of a stronger low near the coast of Washington
state.

The cooling trend will continue into the weekend as extensive
troughing develops off the coast south of the parent low near the
WA coast. Saturday and Sunday will likely be the coolest days,
with high temperatures 10 to 15 degrees below average for inland
areas and 15 to 20 degrees below average for the mountains above
6000 feet. High temperatures on Sunday for the coast and valleys
will be mostly in the 60s, for the mountains in the mid 30s to mid
50s, and for the lower deserts in the lower to mid 70s.

The low pressure systems will also bring chances for
precipitation, starting as early as Friday evening. Rainfall
chances will be greatest for Saturday night into Sunday morning.
There remains a wide range in rainfall estimates. For the 24 hour
period ending Sunday afternoon, chances for rainfall will be
around 45 to 55 percent for 0.50 inch or more from the coast to
the mountains, with a 35 to 40 percent chance for 1 inches or
more. Snow levels will remain high enough so that any
accumulations will only occur on at the highest elevations.

Precip will taper off early next Monday and temperatures will
begin to rise as the low pressure moves east. High temperatures on
Monday and Tuesday will still be a few degrees below normal for
inland areas under a ridge of high pressure.

&&

.AVIATION...
272330Z...VFR conditions prevail through this evening, with
increasing high clouds above 10 kft expected tonight. Intermittent
areas of low clouds based around 1500-2000 ft MSL expected Sunday
morning from 08-15z, mainly for coastal areas. Very patchy coverage
of low clouds for inland valleys. Clouds scatter out by 15-16z with
VFR conditions expected through Wednesday afternoon.

&&

.MARINE...
No hazardous marine conditions are expected through Sunday.

&&

.BEACHES...
High tides during the late mornings will generate significant
coastal flooding this week. Tides will be anywhere from 1.4 to 1.6
feet above astronomical predictions, bringing high tides to 7.7-7.9
feet through Thursday. A Coastal Flood Warning remains in effect
through Thursday afternoon and contains more details. Otherwise,
surf will become elevated (potential for high surf) on Sunday with
the passage of a storm system. More details in the coming days.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.


&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Coastal Flood Warning until 1 PM PDT Thursday for Orange County
     Coastal Areas-San Diego County Coastal Areas.

PZ...None.

&&
