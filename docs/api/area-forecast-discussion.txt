AFDSGX

Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
258 AM PDT Thu Jul 23 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
High pressure aloft will strengthen into the weekend with high
temperatures for inland areas around 10 degrees above average
through Saturday. Nights will be quite warm with low temperatures
for the deserts as much as 10 to 15 degrees above average. Gradual
drying and slight cooling for the early and middle of next week
with high temperatures for the middle of next week still around 5
degrees above average for inland areas. There is some model
guidance that has a brief surge of moisture into the area on
Sunday which would result in more clouds, high temperatures not as
high for some inland areas, and a slight chance of afternoon
thunderstorms for the mountains.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...

.SHORT TERM (Today through Saturday)...
High pressure aloft to the east will expand and strengthen across
southern California into the weekend. High temperatures for today
will warm a few degrees near the coast to as much as 10 to 15
degrees for the inland valleys onto the lower coastal slopes of
the mountains with not much change for Friday and Saturday.

High temperatures for today will be around 5 degrees above average
near the coast and around 8 to 12 degrees above average for inland
areas. High temperatures for today will range from around 80 near
the coast to the upper 90s to 110 for the Inland Empire with 114
to 118 for the lower deserts.

Low temperatures will be quite warm as well for tonight, Friday
night, and Saturday night with low temperatures for the coast and
valleys mostly in the upper 60s to mid 70s, mostly in the 90s for
the lower deserts, and in the mid 70s to mid 80s for the Apple
and Lucerne Valleys. That is around 5 degrees above average for
coastal areas, 5 to 10 degrees above average for the inland
valleys, and 10 to 15 degrees above average for the deserts.

HeatRisk is expected to peak on Friday and Saturday with
widespread extreme HeatRisk for the lower deserts, major to
extreme Heat Risk for the Inland Empire, the Apple and Lucerne
Valleys, and for the San Diego County mountains. Most areas will
have moderate or greater HeatRisk except at the beaches and for
mountain elevations above around 8000 feet.

While high temperatures will remain mostly below record levels,
there could be a large number of daily records tied or broken for
warmest low temperature for Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

The marine layer will remain around 1200 to 1500 feet deep with
night and morning coastal low clouds spreading no farther inland
than the far western valleys.

&&

.LONG TERM (Sunday through Wednesday)...
There are meaningful model differences in the southern extent of
stronger high pressure on Sunday over the southwest states into
northern Mexico and the resulting differences in the amount of
moisture that may spread into portions of southern California.
Those model solutions that maintain stronger high pressure farther
into northern Mexico have less moisture transport into southern
California while those that are weaker with high pressure across
northern Mexico allow for a greater transport of moisture and
clouds into portions of southern California on Sunday.

The current forecast shows less cooling of high temperatures and
less increase in clouds and chances for afternoon thunderstorms
over the mountains for Sunday with continued slow cooling into
Monday. Beyond the moisture surge and resulting cooling of high
temperatures that could occur on Sunday, gradual cooling and
drying will continue into the middle of next week, but with high
temperatures still around 5 degrees above average for inland
areas. High temperatures for next Wednesday would range from the
mid 70s to around 80 near the coast to the 90s to 102 for the
Inland Empire with 108 to 112 for the lower deserts.

High pressure for the end of next week could be even stronger than
for the end of this week.

&&

.AVIATION...
230945Z....Coast/Valleys...Low clouds based near 700-1100 ft MSL
across San Diego County may locally reach areas of Orange County
after 11z. Patchy vis reductions  4-6SM (locally 0-3SM) over
elevated coastal terrain and valleys along the I-15 corridor
clearing mid morning. VCTY KSNA may see brief cigs near 1000 ft MSL
12-15z before clearing.

Clouds scatter out 15-17z. Cigs developing late again after 07z Fri
across San Diego County, with increasing confidence of mostly clear
conditions across much of Orange County. Cigs slightly higher near
1000 ft MSL.

Mountains/Deserts...Mostly clear through the morning. Areas of
flatter cumulus over mountain peaks after 20z Thu. Otherwise, VFR
conditions prevail through the TAF period.

&&

.MARINE...
Northwesterly winds with gusts 20-25 kts in the outer waters near
San Clemente Island to continue this afternoon and evening.
Additionally, a mixed swell will cause steep and choppy seas 6 to 7
feet on Thursday and Friday in the outer waters.

&&

.BEACHES...
A 3 to 4 feet south swell from 190 degrees with 13 second period
will bring surf of 5 to 7 feet to area beaches Thursday through
Sunday. Highest surf will be on south-southwest facing beaches. In
addition to elevated surf, tides are expected to increase starting
Friday peaking at 5.5 to 6.5 feet MLLW by early next week. The
combination of surf and tides will result in minor coastal flooding
during high tides.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.

&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Beach Hazards Statement through Sunday evening for Orange County
     Coastal Areas-San Diego County Coastal Areas.

     Heat Advisory from 10 AM this morning to 8 PM PDT Monday for
     Orange County Coastal Areas-San Diego County Coastal Areas.

     Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT Monday for Coachella Valley-
     San Bernardino and Riverside County Valleys-The Inland
     Empire-San Diego County Deserts-San Diego County Mountains-
     San Gorgonio Pass near Banning.

     Heat Advisory until 8 PM PDT Monday for Orange County Inland
     Areas-Riverside County Mountains-San Bernardino County
     Mountains-San Diego County Valleys-Santa Ana Mountains and
     Foothills.

     Extreme Heat Warning from 10 AM this morning to 8 PM PDT Monday
     for Apple and Lucerne Valleys.

PZ...Small Craft Advisory until 9 PM PDT this evening for Waters from
     San Mateo Point to the Mexican Border Extending 10 to 60 nm
     out including San Clemente Island.


&&
