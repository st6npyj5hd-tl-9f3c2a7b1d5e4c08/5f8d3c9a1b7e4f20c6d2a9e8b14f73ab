AFDSGX

Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
1045 AM PDT Sun Aug 2 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Still very warm conditions are in place with a subtle cooldown
through the middle of the week. There will be increasing
monsoonal moisture for the latter half of the week, with
increasing chances of precipitation, although this looks to be
only a slight chance, with above average temperatures continuing
on into the upcoming weekend.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...


...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION...

Today through tomorrow night...

The latest satellite imagery reveals that the marine layer has
completely inundated all of the coastal areas of both Orange and
San Diego counties, extending up to 10 miles inland for some
portions of the inland areas, and even making its way far enough
east for some areas of patchy fog in and around the I-15 corridor,
so be advised and watch for quickly deteriorating visibilities if
you happen to be traveling through some of these areas of fog
this morning. Ceilings are generally hovering just under 1000 ft
MSL for most locations , and a little lower from where they were
yesterday. Otherwise, mostly clear skies will prevail and set up
for another very warm day with temperatures still running
generally around 5 to 10 degrees above the average for this time
of year. Winds aloft in the mid-levels have began to shift and
will become more southwesterly throughout today, drawing in drier
air along with it. This will help to impede the chance of
afternoon shower and thunderstorm development over the mountains.
The troughing upstream will also begin to influence the area with
slightly lower heights that will begin a gradual cooling trend
that will continue through mid week. There will also begin the
presence of a more predominant onshore flow, and this will likely
help to draw in the marine layer a little sooner, and with better
coverage for tonight. In addition, this is also going to allow for
winds to increase through San Gorgonio Pass, and along the desert
slopes, with gusts up around 40 mph through the early evening
later today.

Tuesday through Saturday...

The pattern remains rather stagnant through mid week, with the
subtle cooling and conditions remaining dry, and gusty winds in the
passes during the afternoon. Thereafter, mid-level winds will begin
to shift back to being from a more easterly direction, with better
positioning of the high pressure center aloft to allow for some of
these monsoonal waves to develop and advect moisture over our
region. Temperatures will also start to gradually warm again during
this period, with increased humidity values and an increasing chance
of getting thunderstorm activity for our mountains and deserts (and
possibly even all the way to the coastal locations, depending on the
propagation of these monsoonal waves as they develop over the
deserts to our southeast and then transition over our region.
Guidance has also been showing an increase in moisture content and
precipitable water going into the beginning of the following
weekend, which may further increase chances of developing
thunderstorms during the afternoon over the mountains. During this
period, the marine layer will likely struggle to develop, given
the increased mid to high level clouds moving overhead, in
addition, with better offshore flow overnight.

&&

.AVIATION...
021745Z....Coasts/Valleys...Most of the low clouds have cleared land
areas with a few lingering around 1,000-1,500ft MSL along the
southern San Diego coast. Low clouds are expected to return 01-03Z
Monday with beginning along coastal San Diego, increasing in
coverage and pushing inland 20 miles through 13Z. Low clouds will
push into Orange County 03-06Z Monday. Ceilings around 700-1,200ft
MSL are expected. VIS restrictions of 3-5SM will be possible where
clouds intersect higher coastal terrain. Low clouds will begin to
clear inland areas 15-16Z Monday and clear the coast 16-17Z.

.Mountains/Deserts...VFR conditions expected through the TAF period
with predominantly clear skies.

&&

.MARINE...
No hazardous marine conditions are expected through Wednesday.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.


&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...Heat Advisory until 8 PM PDT this evening for Orange County
     Coastal Areas-San Bernardino County Mountains-San Diego
     County Coastal Areas.

     Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT this evening for Apple and
     Lucerne Valleys-Orange County Inland Areas-Riverside County
     Mountains-San Bernardino and Riverside County Valleys-The
     Inland Empire-San Diego County Mountains-San Diego County
     Valleys-Santa Ana Mountains and Foothills.

     Extreme Heat Warning until 8 PM PDT Monday for Coachella Valley-
     San Diego County Deserts-San Gorgonio Pass near Banning.

PZ...None.

&&
