FXUS66 KSGX 140007
AFDSGX

Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
507 PM PDT Sun Sep 13 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Cooler weather each day with a deeper marine layer making it
further inland into mid-week. Afternoon temperatures through
Wednesday will generally be 2-5 degrees below normal. High
pressure strengthens late week with warmer temperatures into the
weekend.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...


...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION FOR 00Z TAF PACKAGE...

Mid level water vapor satellite places an upper level low over
Oregon and Washington, poised to continue to move across the
northern portion of CONUS. This low brought an end to the abnormally
hot weather SoCal had experienced the past week, and allowed for the
marine layer to redevelop and thicken this morning. This system also
has aided in tightening the gradient, producing stronger onshore
flow with afternoon gusts today and tomorrow around 35-50 mph
through wind-prone passes and along the desert slopes. While this
low will meander eastward along the border with Canada on Monday,
a second trough will swing down from the PacNW and form a closed
low over southern Oregon and NorCal by Wednesday. This secondary
low will reinforce the cooler weather we`ve had, maintaining a
cooling trend through at least Wednesday. As such, high
temperatures Mon-Wed will hover around 2-5 degrees below normal
with overnight low temperatures also slightly below normal for
everywhere but the deserts which will remain a bit hotter with
slightly better humidity to the east. The marine layer will
continue to deepen and make it further inland each morning into at
least mid-week, with some hi-res guidance indicating it may be
deep enough to sustain some patchy morning drizzle on Tuesday,
although chances of this occurring are low.

The low will weaken a bit as it becomes absorbed in the upper
level pattern and pulled northward later this week. As the low
departs, subsidence over the Pacific to the southwest of San Diego
will increase, with upper level ridging developing over CA Friday
and Saturday. This will return the area to warming trend for the
late week, although guidance is chaotic with the long-range
pattern as the GFS attempts to return the region to troughing
Sunday and beyond while the ECMWF maintains the warmer ridge.

&&

.AVIATION...
140000Z...Coasts/Valleys...There is much less cloud cover across the
California Bight this afternoon, with only stray patches of FEW low
clouds above 2000ft MSL across the coastal areas. However, expect
low clouds to increase in coverage between 02-06z, with widespread
CIGs across San Diego County by 06z Monday. Clouds spread northward
into Orange County through 09z with about 20 miles of inland extent.
Slightly less inland extent expected across the Inland Empire
compared to last night, with only a 30-40% chance of CIGs between 12-
16z. VIS reductions of 4-6 SM in elevated inland valleys in HZ/BR,
and down to 0-5 SM where clouds intersect terrain in the IE and
surrounding foothills. Clouds scatter back out between 15-17z Monday.

Mountains/Deserts...VFR conditions prevail through the TAF period;
typical late afternoon S/W winds gusting 15-25 kts (up to 35 kts
through the Banning Pass) expected, weakening after 06z Monday.

&&

.MARINE...
West-northwest winds expected to gust up to 20 knots in the outer
coastal waters this afternoon. Otherwise, no hazardous marine
conditions are expected through Thursday.

&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...None.
PZ...None.

&&
