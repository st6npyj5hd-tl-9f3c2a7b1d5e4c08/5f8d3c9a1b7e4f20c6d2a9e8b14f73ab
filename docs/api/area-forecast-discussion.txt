Area Forecast Discussion
National Weather Service San Diego CA
453 PM PDT Thu Aug 13 2026

.SYNOPSIS...
Southwest flow aloft will bring drying of monsoonal moisture the
next few days with surface drying for inland areas as well. High
temperatures will continue to cool today, then slowly warm to
around average for next Tuesday.

&&

.DISCUSSION...FOR EXTREME SOUTHWESTERN CALIFORNIA INCLUDING ORANGE...
SAN DIEGO...WESTERN RIVERSIDE AND SOUTHWESTERN SAN BERNARDINO
COUNTIES...


...UPDATED AVIATION DISCUSSION...

Tonight through Saturday...

Low clouds have struggled to dissipate completely this morning under
the blanket of a "baroclinic leaf" transitioning over the region
that is associated with a shortwave trough approaching the area.
This is also allowing for temperatures to be slightly cooler, and
below the seasonal average for this time of year. There is some
moisture still lingering over the region, along with just enough
forcing to maybe allow for an isolated shower/storm or two to pop
off later this afternoon. However, there is not high confidence of
this since a majority of the instability is confined to the east of
the CWA. Nonetheless, an isolated shower/storm or two developing
over the eastern San Bernardino Mountains and then moving out over
the high deserts cannot be ruled out. Some of the guidance does show
the potential of this, although the consensus, especially with the
mid-level flow being out of the southwest, which is not so favorable
as more dry air is becoming advected in gives more credence to
conditions remaining dry for today and through tomorrow. Winds
will also increase later this afternoon across the higher terrain
of possibly 50 mph or greater, and within the passes with possible
gusts approaching 60 mph for Gorgonio Pass. This will also be the
case for tomorrow, except that winds will be slightly lighter
than this afternoon. Temperatures for tomorrow will also be
slightly warmer, yet still below the seasonal average. The marine
layer should fill in better by later this afternoon and into the
evening with the presence of better onshore flow.

Sunday through Thursday...

Through the weekend, there will be a gradual warmup as the ridge to
the east begins to expand back towards the west. Conditions should
remain dry through then, as monsoonal moisture is suppressed
further to the south. Temperatures will continue to warm into the
early half of next week as the ridge further builds, and there
could be pulses of monsoonal waves moving up from the southeast
around the high centered over the desert southwest, which may
increase chances of getting afternoon thunderstorms over our
mountains and deserts during the afternoon hours. Best chances at
this time look to be on Tuesday of next week, although even for
this day, the mid-level flow initially beginning out of the
southeast earlier in the morning turns more southwesterly by later
in the day, with less favorability for development. Temperatures
will return to around average through Wednesday, and then slowly
begin to drop off going into the latter half of next week as the
ridge weakens. Ensembles also reflect similar trends, with mostly
dry conditions likely persisting through the end of next week.

&&

.AVIATION...
140000Z....Coast/Valleys...Patchy low clouds based near 2000 ft MSL
over coastal San Diego County. Clouds will quickly expand in
coverage and push into the San Diego/Orange County coasts and
valleys in the next few hours. Thereafter, clouds move more slowly
into the Inland Empire, eventually filling most of the coastal basin
by 10-12z Fri. Low clouds rise to near 2500 ft MSL and scatter out
of inland areas from 15-18z, but may linger into the early afternoon
for coastal areas, particularly near KSAN.

Regionwide...BKN clouds at/above 15000 feet MSL and unrestricted
vis, clearing west to east after 12Z Friday.

&&

.MARINE...
No hazardous marine conditions are expected through Monday.

&&

.SKYWARN...
Skywarn activation is not requested. However weather spotters are
encouraged to report significant weather conditions.


&&

.SGX WATCHES/WARNINGS/ADVISORIES...
CA...None.
PZ...None.

&&
